# Walkthrough - Play Store Feature Graphic

I have designed a professional **Feature Graphic** (Imagen de funciones) for your Google Play Store listing. This asset is crucial for attracting users and follows Google's recommended specifications (1024x500).

## The Design

The graphic focuses on a clean, dark aesthetic that matches the app's internal "Dark Mode" and uses the primary **Blue Pastel** accent to create a cohesive brand identity.

### Key Elements:
1.  **Minimalist Logo:** An icon group combining a dumbbell and a stopwatch, representing strength and precision timing.
2.  **Bold Typography:** "GYM HELPER" set in a modern, wide-spaced sans-serif font for maximum legibility on mobile store pages.
3.  **Value Propositions:** Three badges highlighting the app's core strengths:
    - **OFFLINE DATABASE:** Emphasizing privacy and no cloud dependency.
    - **HEADPHONE CUES:** Highlighting the smart audio routing feature.
    - **5 ACCENT COLORS:** Showcasing the high level of personalization.

### 2. Converting to PNG (Required for Play Store)
Google Play Store requires a **PNG** or **JPG** file. You can easily convert it:
- **Option A (Online):** Search for "SVG to PNG converter" and upload this file.
- **Option B (Browser):** Open the SVG in your browser, and use a "Screen Capture" tool to grab the 1024x500 area.
- **Option C (Design Software):** Open it in Figma, Adobe Illustrator, or Canva to export it exactly as needed.

> [!TIP]
> Make sure to export it at exactly **1024 x 500 pixels** to meet Google's upload requirements!
